About Us

Helping a generation rise into purpose.

We're the youth & young adult church of MFM Mega Region 2, USA — a family of young people across the region encountering the power of God for discovery, recovery and restoration.

Who We Are

A place to encounter the power of God.

Nobody comes to the Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries by chance. We exist to help youth and young adults discover their identity in Christ, understand the power of prayer, and learn to defend and live out their faith.

Across every campus and city in Mega Region 2, we're raising a generation of young people who love Jesus, live holy, pray with fire, and boldly shine His light everywhere they go.
